
🌍 AQI_Justice — Fight Pollution with Legal Power
"Clean air is not a privilege. It’s a legal right."

AQI_Justice is a civic-tech platform that transforms real-time air pollution data into actionable legal enforcement.
When air quality crosses legal limits, citizens can instantly file evidence-backed complaints against responsible authorities — no legal knowledge required.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/demo-live-success)](https://streamable.com/f6smed)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/GitHub-Repository-blue)](https://github.com/rasaltejas/AQI_Justice)

🚨 Why This Matters
In cities like Delhi, AQI frequently exceeds hazardous and illegal levels, leading to:

*Respiratory diseases
*Heart conditions
*Premature deaths

📌 The laws already exist.
📌 The data already exists.
❌ But citizens lack simple tools to act.

"Pollution is treated as a statistic — not a crime."


💡 Our Solution

AQI_Justice closes the gap between data, law, and citizen action.

*We convert live AQI data into:
⚖️ Legal compliance checks
❤️ Human-readable health impact
📝 One-tap legal complaints


✨ Key Features
📡 Live AQI Monitoring

*Real-time, location-based AQI tracking
*Clear classification (Good → Hazardous)
*Automatic updates


⚖️ Legal Violation Detection (Core Innovation)

*Maps AQI levels to Indian environmental laws

*Instantly flags LEGAL VIOLATIONS

*Identifies responsible authorities:
  *CPCB
  *SPCB
  *NGT

❤️ Health Impact Analysis

*Converts AQI into cigarette-equivalent exposure

*Highlights health risks for:
   *Children
   *Elderly
   *Asthma & heart patients


🛡️ Smart Protection Tips

*Personalized safety recommendations

*Mask guidance (N95)

*Best time for outdoor activity

*Indoor air safety tips


🗺️ Pollution Source Mapping

*Location-based pollution visualization

*Source contribution estimation

*Heatmap insights


📝 One-Tap Legal Complaint Filing

*Auto-generated, evidence-based complaints

*Pre-filled AQI data, location & legal sections

*Complaint lifecycle tracking
